## Focused negative, fault and compatibility checks

- Five finite templates only; reject unknown versions, stages, fields, role substitutions, grant/manifest injection, candidate paths, oversize output and cross-issue evidence ids.
- Full risk never downgrades from role/model text; current role minimum stays focused; full risk and explicit QA rules cannot be skipped.

- Absence reads empty with no writes; malformed/future aggregate retains bytes and fails closed. Bounds, cross-record cycles/owners and stale revisions fail without partial authority.
- Two workflow starts for one issue conflict; concurrent issues in one project still have at most one active writer and current scheduler capacity.
- Crash before/after every admission-intent boundary, lost completion callback and duplicate ticks reconcile the exact same identities or visibly block; no uncommitted stage executes.
- Expired/replaced lease cannot seal a handoff, commit review evidence, reset correction allowance or schedule a successor. Resume checks current task, candidate, grant, source, sandbox and thread contract.
- Root and correction task/attempt/runtime/token caps stop successor admission. Monotonic usage is charged once per run; unknown interrupted usage is not zero; cancellation survives restart.
- New correction starts from prior exact candidate commit; protected probes/policy and original assignment remain unchanged. Replacement/cancellation/retry cannot reset lineage allowance.
- Candidate tree ownership/containment/clean revision is checked before and after Reviewer/QA; stale, modified, missing or active-writer trees fail. Missing legacy trusted receipts are not backfilled.
- Passed verification does not prematurely mark workflow implementation ready; failed/missing functional or strict-TDD receipts cannot be waived by review/risk acknowledgement.
- Manual API/CLI issue dispatch, task retry, review publication and tree cleanup cannot bypass an active workflow. Existing independently authorized non-workflow publication paths remain covered without executing real publication.
- Human scope/owner/criteria/scaffold/publication boundaries cannot be made executable by a handoff recommendation. Cancel preserves evidence and does not silently cancel an unrelated or newer issue.
