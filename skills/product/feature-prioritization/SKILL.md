# Skill: Feature Prioritization

## Objective

Rank work so the team can make progress with the highest useful leverage.

## When To Use

- Selecting the next feature.
- Ordering backlog items.
- Choosing between competing product or engineering investments.

## Procedure

1. Define decision criteria.
2. Score impact, effort, risk, dependency, and urgency.
3. Identify sequencing constraints.
4. Recommend a short ordered list.
5. Call out assumptions that need validation.

## Output Format

- Criteria.
- Ranked options.
- Trade-offs.
- Recommended sequence.
- Dependencies.
- Open questions.

## Limits

- Do not pretend scores are objective when inputs are uncertain.
- Do not prioritize without naming the user or business outcome.
