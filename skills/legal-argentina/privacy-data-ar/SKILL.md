# Skill: Argentine Privacy And Personal Data Review

## Objective

Review personal data handling in an Argentine context at a preliminary level and identify compliance questions for human review.

## When To Use

- Reviewing products, forms, contracts, logs, analytics, databases, or integrations that process personal data.
- Checking privacy notices, consent language, data retention, access control, transfers, and sensitive data handling.

## Procedure

1. Identify data subjects, data categories, sources, purposes, storage locations, and recipients.
2. Flag sensitive data, children's data, health data, financial data, and identity data.
3. Review consent, notice, purpose limitation, retention, security controls, and access rights.
4. Use Law 25.326 as a general reference point only.
5. Mark any requirement that needs updated legal verification.
6. Produce a privacy review draft for human validation.

## Output Format

- Data flow summary.
- Personal data categories.
- Risk areas.
- Missing notices or controls.
- Argentine legal points to verify.
- Questions for legal/security review.
- Recommended next steps.

## Limits

- Do not provide final legal advice.
- Do not invent current regulatory guidance.
- Do not approve production data handling without human review.
