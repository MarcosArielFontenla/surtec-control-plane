# Skill: Argentine Legal Triage

## Objective

Classify a legal situation at a preliminary level and prepare a structured intake for human legal review.

## When To Use

- A user describes a dispute, claim, contract issue, employment issue, consumer issue, privacy issue, or administrative concern.
- Facts are incomplete and the next step is legal intake, not final advice.

## Procedure

1. Separate facts from assumptions.
2. Identify relevant legal areas.
3. List dates, parties, documents, notices, payments, communications, and deadlines mentioned.
4. Identify missing information.
5. Flag urgent risks and evidence-preservation needs.
6. Prepare questions for the lawyer or responsible reviewer.

## Output Format

- Matter summary.
- Likely legal areas.
- Known facts.
- Missing facts and documents.
- Risks and deadlines to verify.
- Questions for human reviewer.
- Suggested next information-gathering steps.

## Limits

- Do not make final legal conclusions.
- Do not invent legal rules.
- Do not calculate legal deadlines without marking them for verification.
- Require human legal review before action.
