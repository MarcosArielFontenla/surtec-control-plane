# Skill: Argentine Contract Review

## Objective

Produce a structured preliminary review of a contract involving Argentine legal context.

## When To Use

- Reviewing draft contracts, terms, amendments, or templates.
- Identifying red flags, missing clauses, jurisdiction issues, consumer implications, employment implications, and data protection concerns.
- Preparing questions for a lawyer or responsible human reviewer.

## Procedure

1. Identify contract type, parties, governing law, jurisdiction, term, payment obligations, and termination mechanics.
2. Extract obligations, rights, deadlines, penalties, warranties, liability caps, confidentiality duties, and data processing clauses.
3. Flag missing or ambiguous clauses.
4. Check whether consumer, employment, privacy, tax, or regulated-industry issues may apply.
5. Mark any legal statement that needs updated verification.
6. Produce a review draft for human legal validation.

## Output Format

- Executive summary.
- Contract classification.
- Key obligations.
- Red flags.
- Missing or weak clauses.
- Argentine legal issues to verify.
- Questions for human review.
- Suggested drafting notes.

## Limits

- Do not provide final legal advice.
- Do not invent statutes, case law, deadlines, or regulatory requirements.
- Do not approve a contract for signature.
- Require human legal review before external use.
